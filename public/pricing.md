# Pricing — Auserene

Auserene is an AI journaling app for iPhone. Prices are in US dollars; the App Store shows the local price in your country. Subscriptions are managed through Apple and can be cancelled any time in your iPhone's subscription settings.

Last updated: 2026-09-27

## Free
- Price: $0, forever
- Notes, as many as you like
- An evening walk through your notes, on your own
- Three "Help me write" nudges a day
- Habit goals and reminders
- People you add yourself
- Guided meditation journeys

## Premium
- Price: $105.99/year (works out to $8.83/month), $11.99/month, or $4.99/week
- Free trial: the first 7 days of the yearly plan are free
- Everything in Free, plus:
  - Evening conversations, and a chat any time of day
  - Memory across every note and chat
  - A meditation made from each day
  - Journal entries written from your conversations
  - Voice notes
  - Remembers the people you mention, on its own

## If you stop paying
Everything you wrote stays yours and stays in the app. Notes, goals and the guided journeys keep working. The chats and the meditations made from your day pause until you come back.
