## Why people look for Day One alternatives

Day One is one of the best-known journal apps there is. Its [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/day-one-daily-journal-diary/id1044867788) had a 4.8 rating from 119K ratings when we checked, it has won Apple's App of the Year, and it runs on almost everything: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Android, Windows and the web ([homepage](https://dayoneapp.com/)). It's owned by Automattic, the company behind WordPress.com. People who have kept a decade of entries in it often love it. Still, people go looking for apps like Day One, usually for one of these reasons.

1. **AI moved into a new top tier.** On March 30, 2026, Day One [renamed Premium to Silver and added Gold](https://dayoneapp.com/blog/introducing-day-one-silver-and-gold/). Silver kept its features and price. Gold, at $74.99 a year, holds the AI features: Daily Chat, Go Deeper prompts, entry summaries, title suggestions and image generation ([pricing](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/)). Day One is plain about why. It says the AI features "cost meaningful resources to build and run." If you were on Premium and want Daily Chat, you now pay $25 a year more.
2. **The free plan is one device.** Basic is free with unlimited text entries and end-to-end encryption, but its comparison table lists "1 device" and one photo per entry. Syncing across devices starts at Silver, $49.99 a year.
3. **The AI memory isn't something you can review in the Apple apps.** Daily Chat builds memories of your routines, relationships and recurring themes. Its [guide](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/ai-features/daily-chat/) says "reviewing or deleting individual memories isn't available in the iPhone, iPad, or Mac apps." You can turn all AI off, but you can't read what it holds about you there.
4. **AI lifts end-to-end encryption for a moment.** Day One's [AI features guide](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/ai-features/ai-features/) says content from end-to-end encrypted journals "is temporarily not end-to-end encrypted while being processed with AI," and that Daily Chat goes through OpenAI. Day One is open about this, and it requires consent first. Some people would still rather not.
5. **Daily usage limits.** Day One's AI features have daily limits "to ensure fair access," and Daily Chat's limit resets 24 hours after your first message. It doesn't publish the number.
6. **No mood tracking or meditation.** Day One is a record of your life. We found no mood tracker, meditation or breathing feature on its pricing page, features page or App Store listing.

If you leave, Day One's [pricing FAQ](https://dayoneapp.com/pricing/) says your entries stay yours, and its [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/day-one-daily-journal-diary/id1044867788) lists export to PDF, plain text and other formats. Diarium and Reflection both say they can import from Day One.

### 1. Auserene: best Day One alternative with AI you can read

Read this section knowing we wrote it. Auserene is an AI journal for iPhone built around a daily loop. In the evening, Auserene reads those notes and holds a short conversation, asking about the parts that seem to matter. The conversation becomes the day's journal entry. Then it can write a guided meditation from that conversation, five, 15 or 25 minutes long, read aloud with your name said the way you say it.

That's the closest thing on this list to Day One's Daily Chat, with two differences that matter if you're leaving Day One. The first is memory. Everything Auserene has learned sits in the You tab in plain sentences: what seems to help you, what matters to you, what gets to you, and threads still open. You can edit or delete any of it. ## How to choose

1. **Decide what you want the AI to do.** Write the entry with you (Auserene, Day One's Daily Chat, Journey's Pal), analyze you (Reflection, Mindsera), or stay out of the way (Diarium, Apple Journal).
2. **Check whether you can read the memory.** An app that remembers you should show you what it remembers and let you fix it.
3. **Know the encryption you're getting.** Day One, Apple Journal and Journey offer end-to-end encryption. Day One lifts it while AI processes your words, and any app that sends text to a cloud AI model has to read it to reply.
4. **Match the platform to your life.** If you write on Windows or Android, Auserene and Apple Journal won't fit.
5. **Check what you can bring with you.** Day One exports your entries; Diarium and Reflection say they import from it.
6. **Try a week of evenings.** Notice whether you close the app calmer or more wound up. That tells you more than any table, this one included.

None of these apps is therapy, ours included. ## Questions people ask about Day One alternatives

### What is the best alternative to Day One?

It depends on why you're leaving. For an AI journal with a meditation made from your day and memory you can edit, Auserene (iPhone only). For a free, private journal on iPhone, Apple Journal. For Day One's range of devices at a lower price, Journey. For no subscription at all, Diarium. For cheap AI on every device, Reflection.

### Is there a free Day One alternative?

Yes. Apple Journal is free, Diarium is free with a one-time Pro upgrade, Reflection has a Free Forever plan, and Auserene's free plan covers notes, mood, habits, people and guided journeys. Day One's own Basic plan is free with unlimited text entries on one device.

### Is Day One's AI only on Gold?

Yes. As of November 2026, Day One's pricing page lists Daily Chat, Go Deeper prompts, entry summaries, title suggestions and image generation under Gold, at $74.99 a year. One Labs feature, AI transcription cleanup, is included with Silver.

### Can you see what Day One's Daily Chat remembers?

Not in the Apple apps. Day One's guide says reviewing or deleting individual memories isn't available in the iPhone, iPad or Mac apps, and it doesn't say whether Android or the web allow it. You can turn AI features off in Settings.

### Is Day One still end-to-end encrypted with AI?

Day One offers end-to-end encryption on every plan. Its AI features guide says content from end-to-end encrypted journals is temporarily not end-to-end encrypted while it's processed by AI, which is one reason AI needs your consent first. Content isn't used to train AI models.

### Can I move my Day One journal to another app?

Day One says you can export your entries, in formats including PDF and plain text. Diarium and Reflection both say they can import from Day One. Check the target app's import options before you cancel.
