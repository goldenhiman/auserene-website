## How to choose

1. **Pick a journal where the parts connect.** Ask whether a note at lunch can shape tonight's conversation, your meditation, a person's page and your goals, or whether each feature sits on its own.
2. **Check whether you can read the memory.** An app that remembers you should show you what it remembers and let you fix it.
3. **Decide what you want the AI to do.** Write the entry with you (Auserene, Day One's Daily Chat, Journey's Pal), analyze you (Reflection, Mindsera), or stay out of the way (Diarium, Apple Journal).
4. **Know the encryption you're getting.** Day One, Apple Journal and Journey offer end-to-end encryption. Day One lifts it while AI processes your words, and any app that sends text to a cloud AI model has to read it to reply.
5. **Match the platform to your life.** If you write on Windows or Android, Auserene and Apple Journal won't fit today. Auserene's Android and web versions are in the works.
6. **Try a week of evenings.** Notice whether you close the app calmer or more wound up. That tells you more than any table, this one included.
