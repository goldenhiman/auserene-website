**5:55 p.m.** Walking to the car, you remember what she told you. You raise your wrist and leave a quick note on your Apple Watch: "Maya's moving to Lisbon in March."

**9:30 p.m.** The evening conversation opens on lunch, because that's what you photographed and where your mood went up. **9:45 p.m.** You make tonight's meditation. Five minutes, ocean waves underneath, your name said the way you say it, and the part about holding on to what matters is about Maya, not a script recorded for millions of people.

**Sunday.** Week in review looks back on all seven days. If "time with old friends" keeps turning up under what seems to help, you turn that insight into a habit goal: someone who keeps in touch, the practice of messaging one old friend, Sunday evening, a finish line of 42 days. You check in with a tap on your phone or your watch. A missed day never turns red.

That's the difference. Day One would have kept the photo, safely, for years. Auserene kept what the day meant and handed it to the next conversation, Maya's page and a habit you'll actually keep.

## Privacy: Day One end-to-end encryption and AI

Day One's [end-to-end encryption FAQ](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/day-one-sync/end-to-end-encryption-faq/) says entries are encrypted with a private key on your device before they reach its servers, and that Day One "never has access to it." That's real protection, on every plan, and it's Day One's clearest win.

AI changes the picture, and Day One says so. Its [AI features guide](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/ai-features/ai-features/) says "content from end-to-end encrypted journals is temporarily not end-to-end encrypted while being processed with AI." Daily Chat goes to a third-party provider, "currently OpenAI," and some features run on the device with Apple Intelligence. Day One says requests aren't kept after processing and entries aren't used to train AI models without explicit permission.

Auserene's [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) says your journal is encrypted with AES-256 at rest, using a key unique to you, and travels over TLS. Our team doesn't read your journal, unless you choose to share a conversation with us in a feedback report. Auserene isn't end-to-end encrypted, because the AI has to read your words to reply. The AI that reads your journal works under zero-data-retention terms, keeps nothing after a reply and is contractually barred from training on your words. No ads, no ad trackers, photos of people stay on your phone, and you can delete everything from inside the app.
