Auserene vs Mindsera comes down to what you want an AI journal to do with your words. Mindsera analyzes them. It comments on your writing through AI "Minds," breaks each entry into themes and emotions, maps your personality on the Big Five, offers more than 52 thinking frameworks, and lets you talk to your journal in a live voice call, on iPhone, iPad, Android, Apple Watch and the web. Auserene, which we make, is built around one evening conversation that reads the notes you left during the day, writes the entry for you, and then turns the day into a guided meditation written from it. Auserene is iPhone only. Both keep a memory of you that you can read and edit.

We make Auserene, so read this with that in mind. ## What is Mindsera?

Mindsera calls itself an "AI journal that reflects back." It comes from Mindsera OÜ in Tallinn, Estonia, and its help center says it launched in March 2023 as "the original AI-powered journal" ([help center](https://mindsera.com/help)). The [homepage](https://mindsera.com/) says it is trusted by over 80,000 users, and its [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mindsera-ai-journal-diary/id6742319153) had a 4.8 rating from 286 ratings when we checked.

The core of Mindsera is analysis. You write, speak or scan a paper page, then bring in AI in several ways.

1. **Go Deeper** asks follow-up questions from "a companion who knows about your past."
2. **Minds** comment on your writing. The [Minds help page](https://mindsera.com/help/editor/minds-comments) lists built-in ones such as Stoic Principles, Alternative Perspectives, Thinking Traps and Past Memories, and you can create your own and pick up to 10 at a time.
3. **Entry Analysis** gives each entry a breakdown of themes, emotions and takeaways.
4. **Insights** look across your whole journal: recurring topics, a Big Five personality profile, suggestions, emotion analysis based on Plutchik's model, and Ask Your Journal, a chat with your entries ([Deep Analysis](https://mindsera.com/help/insights/deep-analysis)).

There's more around that core: morning and evening rituals, a habit tracker, Story View recaps, generated artwork for each entry, a weekly review email, and a directory of frameworks with names like First Principles Thinking, Regret Minimization and Ikigai. Mindsera's [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mindsera-ai-journal-diary/id6742319153) says it is "a self reflection tool and not a substitute for professional mental-health care."

## What is Auserene?

Auserene is an AI journal for iPhone that remembers what helps you. During the day you leave quick notes: a line of text, a photo, a voice note, a scanned page, or a mood on a slider and a Feelings Wheel. In the evening, Auserene reads those notes and holds a short conversation about the day, asking about the parts that seem to matter. The conversation becomes the day's journal entry. Then it can write a guided meditation from that conversation, five, 15 or 25 minutes long, read aloud with your name said the way you say it.

Around that loop sit a chat for any time of day, guided conversations and journeys, habit goals, people pages and a You tab of what it has learned. The full list is on our [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/auserene-ai-journal-habits/id6783458046).

## How do Mindsera and Auserene journal differently?

Mindsera is built for people who like to sit down and think on the page. It gives you a blank editor plus tools you can call in while you write: a framework to structure a decision, a Mind to point out a thinking trap, an analysis when you're done. Its [rituals](https://mindsera.com/help/frameworks/rituals-morningevening), "Set the Day" and "End the Day," are short guided reflections you can customize, and they adapt to your sleep and recovery data if you've connected an Oura ring or Whoop band.

Auserene is built for people who mean to journal and often don't. You leave fragments during the day, and the evening conversation turns them into the entry. Thirteen guided conversations, such as untangle a thought or think a decision through, are there when you want a specific exercise.

If you want your journal to challenge your reasoning and chart your mind, Mindsera has more tools for that than we do. If you want a few minutes of talk at night that leaves you with an entry and something to listen to, that's Auserene.

## Which one remembers you better?

Mindsera's [Memory Profile](https://mindsera.com/help/account/memory-profile) builds a set of bullet-point summaries from your entries, refreshed each night and organized into categories like goals, relationships, habits and patterns ([App Store release notes](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mindsera-ai-journal-diary/id6742319153)). Mindsera says it is "grounded in your entries and doesn't infer or assume things," that you can view, edit and permanently delete it at any time, and that you can switch memory off. It is used by Go Deeper, Minds and Ask Your Journal, and it's a Genius feature ([plan comparison](https://mindsera.com/help/comparison/free-plan-vs-paid-plan)).

Auserene's memory is also on a page you can read. The You tab lists what it has learned in plain sentences: what seems to help you, what matters to you, what gets to you, and threads still open. You can edit or delete any of it. Auserene also keeps track of who's who, so "my sister" always means the same person, and each person in your life has a page with shared moments and insights into how things are between you.

The difference is what the memory feeds. Mindsera's feeds questions and analysis. Auserene's feeds the evening conversation, the chat and the people pages.

## Mood tracking, emotion analysis and patterns

Mindsera's [Deep Analysis](https://mindsera.com/help/insights/deep-analysis) page splits Insights into Patterns (Big Five personality, recurring topics, suggestions), Emotions (Emotional Waves and an emotion breakdown based on Plutchik's framework) and Ask. Insights become available after five days of journaling, Ask Your Journal after three, and Emotional Waves after 21. If you connect an Oura ring or Whoop band, a [Body-Mind](https://mindsera.com/help/insights/body-mind) tab shows recovery and mood side by side.

One oddity we found is that Mindsera's homepage still labels its emotional analysis section "COMING SOON," while its pricing table, help center and App Store release notes describe it as available now.

Auserene works from moods you log. A slider, then a Feelings Wheel with 82 words for what you feel and what caused it, shown month by month. The You tab lists patterns, a week in review looks back on the last seven days, and a heatmap shows your last three months. You can turn any insight into a habit goal.

If you want emotions inferred from your writing and charted, Mindsera does more. If you'd rather name the feeling yourself, Auserene is built that way.

## Voice journaling and Call Mode

Voice is one of Mindsera's strengths. [Voice journaling](https://mindsera.com/help/editor/voice-journaling) transcribes what you say, lets you trigger a follow-up question mid-recording, and keeps the recordings with the entry. You can record from an Apple Watch or a Wear OS watch on Genius. [Call Mode](https://mindsera.com/help/editor/call-mode) is a live voice conversation. You talk, Mindsera responds when you pause, and a single call can last up to 75 minutes. You can choose the voice and the AI's personality.

Mindsera's homepage FAQ says its AI features are "optimized for English."

Auserene's voice notes run up to 10 minutes and are transcribed in over 100 languages, and you can have chat replies read aloud. If a live spoken back-and-forth is what you want, Mindsera's Call Mode is the closer fit.

## Does Mindsera have streaks? Habit tracking compared

Mindsera's free plan includes a habit tracker built around journaling consistency. Its App Store listing mentions streak tracking, smart reminders and weekly targets, and a November update added an option to hide streaks if you'd rather journal without them.

Auserene's habit goals work differently. You start with who you want to be, then pick a practice, a time and a finish line of 21, 42 or 64 days, and tap a dot when you show up. Missed days are never marked red, and there are no streaks. Goals are on the free plan.

## Privacy: Mindsera vs Auserene

Mindsera's [privacy page](https://mindsera.com/help/general/privacy) says writing is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or later, that data is stored in the United States, and that your data "is not used to train or improve AI models." It says end-to-end encryption "is impossible to use with AI at the moment." Its [privacy policy](https://mindsera.com/privacy-policy), effective September 9, 2026, names OpenAI, Anthropic and Google as AI providers. It says Mindsera uses zero data retention for eligible Claude requests, and that retention for OpenAI "depends on the endpoint and our account configuration."

The policy also says Mindsera uses advertising and attribution tools, including the Meta SDK and X Ads, "only with the consent required by law," and that journal content and connected health data "are not used for advertising." Its App Store privacy label lists purchases, contact info, identifiers and usage data under "Data Used to Track You."

Auserene's [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) says your writing is encrypted with a key tied to your account and that no one on our team reads it. Our AI providers work under zero-data-retention contracts, keep nothing after a reply, and are contractually barred from training on your words. There are no ads or ad trackers, photos of people stay on your phone, and you can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

Neither app is end-to-end encrypted, because an AI model has to read your words to reply. Mindsera says so plainly. The useful comparison is which providers see your words, what the contracts say, and what else the app collects.

## Questions people ask about Auserene and Mindsera

### Is Auserene better than Mindsera?

It depends on what you want. Mindsera is stronger on analysis, frameworks, live voice calls, integrations and platforms (Android, iPad, Apple Watch, web). Auserene is stronger if you want an evening conversation that writes the entry and a meditation made from your day. Auserene is iPhone only.

### Is Mindsera free?

Mindsera has a free Curious plan with the journal, habit tracker, frameworks and export. AI features, including memory, voice journaling and Call Mode, need Genius, though free members can try most of them once or a few times.

### How much does Mindsera cost?

As of November 19, 2026, Mindsera Genius costs $14.99 a month or $129 a year on its website. Its App Store listing also shows a $99 annual offer and a $17.99 monthly offer. Students, people over 65 and people with disabilities can ask for 40% off.

### Does Mindsera remember past entries?

Yes, on Genius. Its Memory Profile is a set of summaries built from your entries and refreshed each night, and Mindsera says you can view, edit, delete or switch it off. Auserene's memory works the same way in that respect. Everything it has learned is on the You tab, and you can edit or delete it.

### Does Mindsera have guided meditation?

Mindsera doesn't list a meditation or breathing feature on its site, help center or App Store listing. Auserene writes a guided meditation from each day's conversation and has eight guided meditation journeys and 11 ambient sounds.

### What is a good Mindsera alternative for iPhone?

Auserene, if you want a conversation instead of analysis and a meditation made from your day. Rosebud, if you want guided, therapy-informed journals and don't mind paying from day one. Apple Journal, if you want something free and end-to-end encrypted with no AI chat. Our [best AI journal apps](/blog/best-ai-journal-apps) guide compares more.
