## What is Mindsera?

Mindsera calls itself an "AI journal that reflects back." Around that sit morning and evening rituals, a habit tracker, Story View recaps, generated artwork, a weekly review email and frameworks like First Principles Thinking and Ikigai. The [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mindsera-ai-journal-diary/id6742319153) says it is "a self reflection tool and not a substitute for professional mental-health care." Neither is Auserene.

## Privacy: Mindsera vs Auserene

Auserene's [privacy policy](/privacy-policy) says your journal is encrypted with AES-256 at rest, using a key unique to you, and travels over TLS. Mindsera's [privacy page](https://mindsera.com/help/general/privacy) says writing is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or later, stored in the United States, and "is not used to train or improve AI models." It says end-to-end encryption "is impossible to use with AI at the moment." Its [privacy policy](https://mindsera.com/privacy-policy), effective September 9, 2026, names OpenAI, Anthropic and Google as AI providers, uses zero data retention for eligible Claude requests, and says OpenAI retention "depends on the endpoint and our account configuration." Its App Store privacy label lists purchases, contact info, identifiers and usage data under "Data Used to Track You."

Neither is end-to-end encrypted. Any journal that sends your words to an AI has to process them at that moment. The useful comparison is which providers see your words, what the contracts say, and what else the app collects.
