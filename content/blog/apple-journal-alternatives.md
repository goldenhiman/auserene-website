## What Apple Journal does well

Journal is [free on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/journal/id6447391597) for iPhone and iPad (iOS 17.2 or later), and it had 4.8 stars from 345K ratings when we checked. Apple now publishes a [Journal user guide for Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/journal/welcome/mac) covering macOS Tahoe 26 and macOS 27, and entries [sync across iPhone, iPad and Mac](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/protect-your-journal-entries-iph9c59b1557/ios) through iCloud.

Apple's [Journaling Suggestions privacy page](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/journaling-suggestions/) says suggestions use on-device processing to group your workouts, photos, places, music and people into moments to write about. It also says that with default two-factor authentication and a passcode, "all Journal entries are end-to-end encrypted when stored in iCloud, so even Apple can't read them." Apple's [iCloud data security overview](https://support.apple.com/en-us/102651) lists Journal data as end-to-end encrypted even under standard data protection.

It also has multiple journals, a Places map, search and bookmarks ([guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/view-and-search-journal-entries-iph6257be047/ios)), Insights with streaks and reminders ([guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/build-a-journaling-habit-iph70107aec2/ios)), state of mind logging that goes to the Health app ([guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/journal-for-your-wellbeing-iph7b79617d5/ios)), PDF export ([guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/print-and-export-entries-iph4cad323fe/ios)) and a Face ID lock. That's a lot for free.

## Why people look for an Apple Journal alternative

1. **It doesn't talk back.** Journal suggests moments and, on iOS 27, writing prompts. It doesn't hold a conversation about your day, summarize your week or remember what you told it last month.
2. **It's Apple-only.** Apple's App Store listing and user guides cover iPhone, iPad and Mac. If you also use Android, Windows or a work laptop's browser, there's no Journal there.
3. **Voice transcription is English only.** Apple's guide says audio transcription "is available in English only (excluding Singapore and India locales)" ([source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/add-formatting-photos-and-more-iph492ee70a8/ios)).
4. **The new AI prompts need a recent iPhone.** Writing Prompts with Apple Intelligence need an iPhone 15 Pro or later, an adult account and a supported language ([source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/write-in-your-journal-iph9824e83ce/ios)).
5. **No meditation or guided content.** Journal has prompts and suggestions, but no guided journals, meditations or breathing.

## Does Apple Journal have AI?

Some, and less than the word usually suggests. There are two pieces.

**Journaling Suggestions** group your activity into moments to write about, and Apple says the data used is "private and stored on your iPhone" ([source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-journal-settings-iphf965002cf/ios)).

**Writing Prompts** are newer. Apple's iOS 27 guide says that "with Apple Intelligence, Journal can suggest customized writing prompts based on what you write and any photos or drawings you add to your entry," and you can add up to three prompts to an entry ([source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/write-in-your-journal-iph9824e83ce/ios)). The iOS 26 version of that page doesn't mention them, so they look new in iOS 27. Apple's Journal pages don't say whether the prompts are generated on the device or in Apple's cloud.

What we didn't find in Apple's Journal guide is a chat, a summary of your entries, or any memory of what you've written across entries. If you're looking for an Apple Journal alternative with AI that responds to you, that's the gap the apps below fill.

## How to choose

1. **Decide what the AI should do, if anything.** Nothing (Apple Journal, Diarium), talk with you and write the entry (Auserene, Day One's Daily Chat), coach and review (Reflection, Rosebud), or analyze you (Mindsera).
2. **Check whether you can read the memory.** Auserene lets you read and edit everything it remembers. Mindsera's [Memory Profile](https://mindsera.com/help/account/memory-profile) lets you view, edit or delete what it remembers. Day One doesn't let you review Daily Chat memories in its Apple apps. The others don't state it.
3. **Weigh end-to-end encryption honestly.** Apple Journal has it by default. Day One and Journey offer it, and Day One's AI works outside it. Any app that replies to your words with a cloud AI model has to read them to do it.
4. **Match the platform to your life.** If you write on Android or Windows, Apple Journal and Auserene won't fit.
5. **Try a week of evenings.** Notice whether you close the app calmer or more wound up. That tells you more than any table.

## Questions people ask about Apple Journal alternatives

### What is the best alternative to Apple Journal?

It depends on what you're missing. For an AI that talks with you, remembers you and writes a meditation from your day, Auserene (iPhone only). For a long archive on every device with optional AI, Day One. For a cheap AI coach on Android and the web too, Reflection. For a private diary with a one-time price, Diarium. For meditation and routines, Stoic.

### Does Apple Journal have AI?

A little. Journaling Suggestions use on-device processing to suggest moments to write about, and on iOS 27, Writing Prompts with Apple Intelligence suggest prompts based on what you write, on an iPhone 15 Pro or later. Apple's Journal guide doesn't describe any chat, summaries or memory of past entries.

### Is there an Apple Journal alternative with AI?

Yes. Auserene, Day One (Gold plan), Rosebud, Reflection, Mindsera and Stoic all use AI to respond to your entries. Auserene and Day One turn a conversation into the entry; Reflection and Rosebud coach and review; Mindsera analyzes.

### Is Apple Journal better than Day One?

Apple Journal is free, simpler and end-to-end encrypted by default. Day One costs money for more than one device but runs on Android, Windows and the web, holds more media per entry, and adds AI chat on its Gold plan. Day One can import your Apple Journal entries.

### Is Apple Journal available on Android or Mac?

It's on the Mac since macOS Tahoe 26, and syncs with iPhone and iPad through iCloud. It isn't available on Android, Windows or the web.

### Is Apple Journal private?

Apple says that with default two-factor authentication and a passcode, all Journal entries are end-to-end encrypted in iCloud, so even Apple can't read them, and that Journaling Suggestions are processed on your device.
