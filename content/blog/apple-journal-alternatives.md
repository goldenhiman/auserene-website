## What Apple Journal does well

Apple's [Journaling Suggestions privacy page](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/journaling-suggestions/) says suggestions use on-device processing, and that with default two-factor authentication and a passcode, "all Journal entries are end-to-end encrypted when stored in iCloud, so even Apple can't read them." ## Why people look for an Apple Journal alternative

1. **It doesn't talk back.** Journal suggests moments and, on iOS 27, writing prompts. It doesn't hold a conversation about your day, summarize your week or remember what you told it last month.
2. **Nothing connects.** Your mood goes to Health and your entries stay in Journal. Apple's guides describe no meditation, no page for the people in your life and no goals beyond a streak.
3. **It's Apple-only.** Apple's listing and guides cover iPhone, iPad and Mac. No Android, Windows or web.
4. **Voice transcription is English only.** Apple's guide says audio transcription "is available in English only (excluding Singapore and India locales)" ([source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/add-formatting-photos-and-more-iph492ee70a8/ios)).
5. **The new AI prompts need a recent iPhone.** Writing Prompts with Apple Intelligence need an iPhone 15 Pro or later, an adult account and a supported language ([source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/write-in-your-journal-iph9824e83ce/ios)).

## Does Apple Journal have AI?

Some, and less than the word usually suggests.

**Journaling Suggestions** group your activity into moments to write about, and Apple says the data used is "private and stored on your iPhone" ([source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/change-journal-settings-iphf965002cf/ios)).

**Writing Prompts** are newer. Apple's iOS 27 guide says that "with Apple Intelligence, Journal can suggest customized writing prompts based on what you write and any photos or drawings you add to your entry," up to three per entry ([source](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/write-in-your-journal-iph9824e83ce/ios)). Apple doesn't say whether the prompts are generated on the device or in its cloud.

What we didn't find in Apple's Journal guide is a chat, a summary of your entries, or any memory across entries. If you want an Apple Journal alternative with AI that responds to you, that's the gap the apps below fill.

## How to choose

1. **Decide whether you want tools or one connected journal.** Most apps here give you a diary plus an extra or two. In Auserene each piece hands off to the next: notes to conversation, conversation to entry and meditation, all of it to memory, people and goals.
2. **Decide what the AI should do, if anything.** Nothing (Apple Journal, Diarium), talk with you and write the entry (Auserene, Day One's Daily Chat), coach and review (Reflection, Rosebud), or analyze you (Mindsera).

## Questions people ask about Apple Journal alternatives

### What is the best alternative to Apple Journal?

If you want one journal where everything connects, Auserene is the stronger pick. It connects quick notes, an evening conversation that writes the entry, a meditation made from your day, memory you can read and edit, people and habit goals, on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch, with a free plan. For a long archive on every device, Day One. For a cheap AI coach on Android and the web, Reflection. For a private diary with a one-time price, Diarium.

### Does Apple Journal have AI?

A little. Journaling Suggestions use on-device processing to suggest moments to write about, and on iOS 27, Writing Prompts with Apple Intelligence suggest prompts based on what you write, on an iPhone 15 Pro or later. Apple's Journal guide doesn't describe any chat, summaries or memory of past entries.

### Is there an Apple Journal alternative with AI?

Yes. Auserene turns an evening conversation into your entry and a meditation, and remembers what matters in a way you can read and edit. Day One (Gold plan), Rosebud, Reflection, Mindsera and Stoic also use AI to respond to your entries.

### Is Apple Journal better than Day One?

Apple Journal is free, simpler and end-to-end encrypted by default. Day One costs money for more than one device but runs on Android, Windows and the web, holds more media per entry, and adds AI chat on its Gold plan. Day One can import your Apple Journal entries.

### Is Apple Journal available on Android or Mac?

It's on the Mac since macOS Tahoe 26, and syncs with iPhone and iPad through iCloud. It isn't available on Android, Windows or the web.

### Is Apple Journal private?

Yes. Apple says that with default two-factor authentication and a passcode, all Journal entries are end-to-end encrypted in iCloud, so even Apple can't read them, and that Journaling Suggestions are processed on your device.
