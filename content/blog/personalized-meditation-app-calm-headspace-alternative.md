## What makes a meditation app personalized?

Most apps say they personalize. It helps to ask which kind. Our [personalized meditation](/blog/personalized-meditation) piece sorts it into four levels.

1. **You pick from a library.** Sleep or focus, five or 20 minutes. This is what most apps mean.
2. **A questionnaire picks for you.** You answer a few questions and rules route you to a track.
3. **The session adapts to signals.** Pacing, length or sound changes based on what you report or what a sensor reads.
4. **The words are written for you.** The script comes from what you've said, so it can mention what actually happened today.

Calm and Headspace mostly work at the first level, with Ebb adding a conversation that leads to a recommendation. Balance works at the second and third. Endel works at the third, for sound. Auserene works at the fourth, and it's the only level where the meditation can mention your Tuesday. It's also the newest and has the least direct evidence, so try it for a week and judge for yourself.
