## How to choose

1. **Decide whether you want the pieces connected.** If you want your notes, conversation, meditation, memory, people and goals to feed each other, that's Auserene. Most others keep each feature on its own.
2. **Check whether you can read the memory.** An app that remembers you should show you what it remembers and let you fix it. Auserene, Mindsera (its [Memory Profile](https://mindsera.com/help/account/memory-profile)) and ChatGPT let you see and edit what they remember; Day One doesn't in its Apple apps; the others don't state it.
3. **Decide what you want the AI to do.** Ask questions and write with you (Auserene, Day One's Daily Chat, ChatGPT), analyze you (Mindsera, Reflection), or speak in many voices (Life Note).
4. **Find the training default.** Most journal apps here say your entries don't train AI. ChatGPT does by default unless you opt out.
5. **Know the encryption you're getting.** Apple Journal and Day One offer end-to-end encryption, and Day One lifts it for AI. Every app that sends your words to a cloud AI model has to read them to reply.
6. **Check the free plan's terms, today.** Rosebud's ended this year. Read what a plan includes now and whether you can export.
7. **Match the platform to your life.** If you write on a laptop or an Android phone, an app made for iPhone and iPad won't fit.
8. **Try a week of evenings.** Notice whether you close the app calmer or more wound up. That tells you more than any table, this one included.
